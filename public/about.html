About PENZO

PENZO is an independent, non-governmental accessibility platform designed to connect examination candidates who require scribe assistance with compassionate, qualified volunteer scribes.

Our Mission

Every eligible candidate deserves equal access to examination opportunities without administrative bottlenecks or the last-minute stress of finding an eligible scribe. PENZO bridges this gap through verified listings, transparent availability matching, and mutual coordination.

Non-Affiliation & Regulatory Disclaimer

Important Notice: PENZO is an independent coordination tool and is not an official government portal, nor is it affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing any government examination authority or commission. All rules, quotas, approvals, and scribe eligibility determinations remain solely under the authority of the relevant conducting commission (e.g., UPSC, SSC, State PSCs, NTA). Matches proposed by PENZO are purely potential matches subject to mutual agreement and official approval.
