Help & Frequently Asked Questions

What is PENZO?

PENZO is an independent web application that helps candidates who require scribes for examinations connect with volunteer scribes.

Who can request a scribe?

Candidates with benchmark disabilities as defined under relevant guidelines who are appearing for competitive, academic, or entrance examinations.

Are volunteer matches guaranteed to be approved?

No. PENZO facilitates community coordination only. You must verify and submit the necessary scribe undertakings and documents to your official examination centre as required by the conducting authority.
