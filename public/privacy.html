Privacy Policy

PENZO respects and protects the privacy of candidates and volunteers. This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Data Minimization

We collect only the information necessary for coordinating scribe assistance: mobile number, name, preferred examination location, languages, and general assistance needs. We do not collect unnecessary sensitive identity documents or private medical diagnoses.

2. Security & Storage

Mobile numbers are masked in user-facing views. Passwords and authentication challenges are cryptographically hashed. Sessions are managed using secure, HttpOnly cookies.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Relevant coordination details are shared only between mutually confirmed candidates and volunteers for examination day preparation.
