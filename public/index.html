How PENZO Works

Coordination made simple, dignified, and compliant with examination guidelines.

1. Browse Examinations

Explore verified government examination listings, schedules, sessions, and specific scribe requirements.

2. Connect & Match

Candidates submit assistance requests while volunteers express availability in their local areas and languages.

3. Confirm Coordination

Transparent review, invitation acceptance, and mutual confirmation for complete peace of mind on exam day.

Important Disclaimer

PENZO is an independent community coordination initiative. PENZO does not claim to be an official government portal nor does it guarantee that a volunteer match is approved by an examination authority. Platform-generated matches are potential matches only; final eligibility and approval remain strictly subject to the respective examination body's rules.
