How PENZO Works

Examination coordination made simple, dignified, and fully aligned with examination authority guidelines.

Step 1

Browse Examinations

Explore verified government and competitive examination listings, schedules, session timings, and specific scribe eligibility criteria.

Step 2

Connect & Match

Candidates submit structured assistance requests while qualified volunteers register availability in their local areas and languages.

Step 3

Confirm Coordination

Transparent profile verification, coordinator review, and mutual commitment for complete confidence and peace of mind on exam day.

Important Disclaimer

PENZO is an independent community coordination initiative. PENZO does not claim to be an official government portal nor does it guarantee that a volunteer match is approved by an examination authority. Platform-generated matches are potential matches only; final eligibility and approval remain strictly subject to the respective examination body's rules.
